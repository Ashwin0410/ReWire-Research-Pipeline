Informed Consent

What this study is

You'll listen to a short audio piece and answer questions about how it made you feel, including whether you experienced chills, goosebumps, or emotional responses. Some questions ask about your moods, your inner thoughts, and personal experiences. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question you don't want to answer.

Who can take part

You must be 18 or older and located in the United States.

How we use your responses

This is a commercial research study run by ReWire Neurotechnologies, Inc. Your responses, including written answers and your reactions to the audio, will be used to develop, test, and improve our products, including the AI systems that generate personalized audio. We may retain and reuse your responses for these purposes over time.

Personal information

We collect your Prolific ID to manage participation and compensation, and we remove it before using your data for product development.

Storage and security

Your responses are stored securely and accessed only by ReWire and people working on its behalf. They may be shared with research or commercial partners only in de-identified or aggregated form.

Withdrawal

Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time. This study is not therapy, not medical treatment, and not a substitute for professional care. If you feel distressed, you can stop immediately, and resources will be provided at the end. If you are in crisis, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988).
