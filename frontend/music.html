Thanks for participating in our study!

This study explores how audio can create powerful emotional responses called emotional chills.

Chills (also called "aesthetic chills" or "frisson") are a strong emotional reaction accompanied by a shiver, tingling, or feeling of cold down the spine. They are sometimes but not necessarily accompanied by goosebumps. Not everyone experiences them, and that is completely normal.

In this study, you will listen to a short audio piece (~10 minutes) and answer questions about your experience.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses are anonymous and will only be used for research purposes.

Please have headphones ready before you continue.
